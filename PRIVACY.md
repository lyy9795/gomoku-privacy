# Privacy Policy — Gomoku Master

Gomoku Master does not collect, transmit, or share any user data.

- No analytics or telemetry of any kind.
- No network requests are made by the extension.
- The only stored data is a local win/loss/draw counter and the selected
  difficulty, kept in `chrome.storage.local` on your own machine.
- Uninstalling the extension removes this data.
